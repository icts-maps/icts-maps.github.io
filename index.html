The Nexus of Theoretical Sciences

Welcome to the Mathematics and Physics society (MAPs) at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences. We are an interdisciplinary forum dedicated to the rigorous exploration of foundational reality.

From the emergent behavior of complex systems to the mathematical symmetry underpinning quantum mechanics, MAPs provides a dedicated space where the abstraction of mathematics meets the phenomena of physics.

Interdisciplinary Synthesis

We actively break down the boundaries between theoretical physics, computational biology, and abstract mathematics.

Active Deconstruction

Our sessions prioritize blackboard derivations and conceptual translation over passive, formal presentations.

Our Intellectual Philosophy

Our emblem—the broken symmetry—represents the fundamental truth of the universe we study. In pure mathematics and absolute physics, perfect symmetry is a void. It is only when symmetry breaks that time, matter, and complexity emerge.

MAPs exists exactly at this conceptual fault line. We study the pure, abstract frameworks of reality (the upper gold arc) and trace how they map onto chaotic, physical phenomena (the lower physical arc). The observer, and this society, acts as the central point bridging the two.
